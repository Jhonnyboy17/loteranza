## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a futuristic, high-stakes aesthetic engineered for premier international lottery courier platforms. It projects exclusivity, technical precision, and absolute transparency. The experience evokes the adrenaline of multi-million-dollar draws combined with the calculated security of institutional fintech.

The style operates at the intersection of **Futuristic Minimalism** and **Dark Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmospheric Depths:** Pure deep-space grafite canvas (#090A0F) establishes absolute focus, pushing extraneous noise into complete shadow.
- **Electric Accentuation:** Laser violet emission (#8B5CF6) guides operational flow and active states, while high-voltage amber (#FACC15) commands attention for monumental jackpot values and winning allocations.
- **Optic Glass & Luminescence:** Translucent panels featuring 1px micro-borders, delicate gradient rim lights, and soft localized luminescence produce depth without visual clutter.
- **Numeric Monumentality:** Data takes center stage. Winning digits, countdown intervals, and currency metrics are treated as visual heroes, stripped of superfluous ornament.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture relies on an ink-dark foundation that permits neon emitters to vibrate with high contrast and clarity:

- **Base Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas / Background`: `#090A0F` (abyssal graphite, eliminates backlight bleed)
  - `Surface Low`: `#12141F` (secondary containment and structural layering)
  - `Surface Card / Glass`: `rgba(24, 27, 42, 0.65)` with backdrop filter
  - `Surface Elevate`: `#1E2238` (dropdowns, tooltips, interactive overlays)

- **Primary Violet Series (System & Interaction):**
  - `Primary Focus`: `#8B5CF6` (action targets, active lottery selections)
  - `Primary Vivid`: `#A855F7` (hover illumination, interactive glows)
  - `Primary Deep`: `#6D28D9` (borders, gradient endpoints, toggles)

- **Secondary Gold / Amber (Jackpot Currency & Wins):**
  - `Accent Gold`: `#FACC15` (multi-million headline figures, mega ball multipliers)
  - `Accent Amber`: `#EAB308` (active jackpot counters, countdown alerts)
  - `Gold Glow`: `rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.18)` (ambient halo around prize figures)

- **Functional Status:**
  - `Success / Confirmed Ticket`: `#10B981`
  - `Critical / Draw Closing`: `#EF4444`
  - `Info / Ball Counter`: `#06B6D4`

- **Typography Tiers:**
  - `Text Hero`: `#FFFFFF` (100% white for jackpot metrics and active digits)
  - `Text Body`: `#E2E8F0` (soft off-white for operational readability)
  - `Text Muted`: `#94A3B8` (subtitles, draw metadata, currency tags)
  - `Text Subtle`: `#475569` (disabled states, inactive grid numbers)

## Typography

The typographic system balances the geometric velocity of **Outfit** with the technical clarity of **Inter**:

- **Outfit (Headlines, Numerics, Badges):** Built with open apertures and modernist circular geometry. Utilized for jackpot balances, prize titles, time intervals, and lottery sphere indicators. Font weights 700 and 800 deliver the physical authority required for astronomical dollar amounts.
- **Inter (Body, Legal, Ticket Disclosures):** Chosen for uncompromised legibility against dark luminous backdrops. Its uniform letterforms neutralize eye fatigue across order reviews, rules, and confirmation steps.
- **Monospaced Numerical Alignments:** All lottery spheres, countdown timers, and rolling odometer numbers must apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter during draw countdown updates and value tickers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an expansive, unhurried structural cadence designed to frame high-value lotteries with museum-grade focus:

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (max container `1360px`), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 
- **Spacious Breathing Room:** Jackpot showcases demand isolation. Hero modules utilize `space-xl` internally to separate multi-million digits from ticket actions, preventing cognitive clutter.
- **Number Matrix Layout:** Ball-selection ticket grids adapt dynamically to 5 to 7 columns with precise `space-sm` gaps, ensuring fingertip accuracy without overlap on touch viewports.
- **Breakpoint Structure:**
  - `Mobile (<640px)`: Full-width stacked translucent cards, pinned bottom checkout summary bar, dense ball grids.
  - `Tablet (641px - 1024px)`: 2-column distribution for lottery options (Powerball, Mega Millions) with side-by-side slips.
  - `Desktop (>1025px)`: Full 12-column cockpit layout; left primary display for play slips, right dedicated 4-column glass rail for digital courier tracking, live draw monitors, and order commitment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through atmospheric layers, glass refraction, and radial luminescence rather than traditional opaque shadows:

- **Level 0 (Abyssal Void):** Raw background `#090A0F` paired with low-opacity radial gradient spheres of violet (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`) and gold (`rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.04)`) placed strategically behind jackpot amounts.
- **Level 1 (Translucent Paneling):** 
  - Background: `rgba(18, 20, 31, 0.7)`.
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces & Slips):**
  - Background: `rgba(24, 27, 42, 0.85)`.
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 40px -8px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15), 0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Victory Overlays):**
  - Background: `rgba(18, 20, 31, 0.95)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.4)`.
  - Shadow: `0 0 50px rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.15), 0 20px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:

- **Cards & Slips:** Curvature set to `0.5rem` (`8px`) for compact operational units and `1rem` (`16px`) for primary lottery card containers. This provides structural precision and a crisp tech-hardware feel without harsh brutalist corners.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Standard boundary of `0.5rem` (`8px`). Secondary micro-tags utilize `0.375rem` (`6px`).
- **Lottery Spheres & Token Indicators:** Spheres, mega-ball multipliers, and quick-pick chips retain pure circle geometries (`border-radius: 9999px`) to mirror actual physical drawing balls while contrasting against rectangular glass cards.

## Components

### 1. Hero Jackpot Display
- **Visuals:** Massive horizontal lockup featuring glowing `#FACC15` text, subtle radial gold aura behind digits, and monospace live countdown clock.
- **Micro-Copy:** Draw name tagged with an uppercase badge (`Outfit`, `label-md`), accompanied by officially licensed courier confirmation checks.

### 2. Lottery Number Spheres (Ball Grid)
- **Inactive / Unselected:** Neutral circular disk (`#181B2A`), border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#94A3B8`.
- **Selected Ball:** Primary gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #A855F7, #7C3AED)`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), outer glow `0 0 14px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.6)`.
- **Powerball / Mega Ball:** Amber gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FDE047, #CA8A04)`), text `#090A0F` (high-contrast black), outer glow `0 0 16px rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.7)`.

### 3. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA ("Play Now" / "Confirm Order"):** Linear gradient (`#8B5CF6` to `#7C3AED`), hover states increase brightness with an active neon rim. Font `Outfit`, `label-lg`, uppercase tracking.
- **Gold CTA ("Claim Jackpot" / "Mega Play"):** High-visibility `#FACC15` background with deep graphite `#090A0F` text.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent surface, border 1px solid `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`, text `#E2E8F0`. Hover triggers `background: rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1)`.

### 4. Translucent Cartela (Play Slip Card)
- **Structure:** Modular container featuring 5 white-ball slots and 1 special-ball slot.
- **Header:** Quick-pick magic wand button, trash action, and line identifier (`Line A`, `Line B`).
- **Surface:** Glassmorphic dark slate with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and inset top-edge highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

### 5. Form Fields & Quick Pick Chips
- **Inputs:** Dark graphite fill (`#12141F`), border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, active focus border `#8B5CF6` with `0 0 0 2px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`.
- **Quick Pick Chips:** Pill-shaped capsules with neon purple border accents. Tap activates haptic animation and randomizes numbers with animated slot-machine shuffle effect.

### 6. Courier Status Bar (Physical Ticket Scan Guarantee)
- **Progress Track:** Monoline segment depicting: `Order Placed` -> `Ticket Purchased in USA` -> `High-Res Scan Uploaded` -> `Stored in Vault`.
- **Scan Preview:** Miniature glass card embedding encrypted serial numbers and authenticated watermark.
